The person behind the work

Educator, author, researcher, civil servant, Christian ministry leader and entrepreneur.

Falade Solomon Adeyemi is a Nigerian educator, author, researcher, civil servant, Christian ministry leader and entrepreneur. His professional and intellectual interests encompass education, Christian spirituality, governance, public administration, social development, youth empowerment and entrepreneurship.

Adeyemi is married to Falade Deborah Olayemi, and they are blessed with two sons, Dominion and Dunamis.

His guiding philosophy centres on education, faith, knowledge, integrity and purposeful living. Through writing, teaching, research, ministry and entrepreneurship, he seeks to make meaningful contributions to individuals, institutions and society.
